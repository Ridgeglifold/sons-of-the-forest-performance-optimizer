## 📖 What Is This Tool?

**Sons of the Forest Performance Optimizer** is a **safe, reversible tuning tool** that helps you squeeze more FPS out of Sons of the Forest without permanently modifying the game. It adjusts graphics settings, engine configs, and Windows-level tweaks — all with automatic backups so you can revert anytime.

Whether you're on a mid-range laptop or a gaming desktop, this tool helps you find the **best balance between visual quality and smoothness**.
